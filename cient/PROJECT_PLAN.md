# Tisha Cafe site sequence

Design and functionality for each item will follow the user's individual briefs. This is the build queue, not a commitment to unapproved features.

| Stage | Deliverables | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Research foundation | Source-aware business constants, menu snapshot, asset inventory, logo palette, dark colors | Prepared; image originals pending |
| 1. Shared shell | Header, footer, routing, responsive light/dark theme, motion conventions, island navigation | Preview built |
| 2. Home | Hero, visit-planning details, card listing, gallery, voice carousel, FAQ | Preview built; real media pending |
| 3. Store flow | Search, menu categories, product details, liked items, Redux cart | Frontend built; checkout integration and café menu approval pending |
| 4. About | Brand story, gallery, ambience and verified identity | Awaiting brief and café facts |
| 5. Contact | Address, directions, phone, hours and contact flow | Awaiting brief and hours confirmation |
| 6. Account | Guest preferences in bottom sheet; sign-up and login after data handling is specified | Guest sheet built; authentication pending |
| 7. Legal | Privacy policy and terms based on actual site functions and approved business details | Awaiting brief |

Use Framer Motion for animated components and Tailwind utilities for all styling. Avoid publishing unsupported facts or treating a third-party menu as the café's approved live catalog.
